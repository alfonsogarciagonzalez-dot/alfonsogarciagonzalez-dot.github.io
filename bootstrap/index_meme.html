Meme Mashup 2026

This image was created in September 19, 2026. This mashup of different images is meant to represent us as art students and our frustration when it comes to working with technology/photoshop. I'm sure there has been many times in our lives where we often use technology and it doesn't go how we plan it to. We see error pop ups, applications crashing, or even data corrupting. For this project, I wanted to use the image of a lady yelling at a cat, to show this strong emotion.
