Lost In The Woods 2026

This image was created in October 11, 2026. For this project, I wanted to work with a suspenseful form of story telling by implementing decisions where the player must decide what path to choose and what consequences they bring. I choose to involve something we are all familiar, which was our class. The story of this small game is to try to find your friends from Art 74 after losing them in a field trip to the forest. As you search for them, you see new environments and must choose between what paths/ actions to take. Hope you guys enjoy!
