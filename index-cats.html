Do you love cats? Learn about these fun facts about cats!

1. Cats are sleepy pets

Cats can sleep for an estimate of 13 to 16 hours per day! As a result of being natural predators, cats require a long peroid of rest before they can become active again, even while playing with their favorite toy!

2. Some Cats Have a Total of 18 Toes

Most domestic cats have 5 toes on the front of their paws and 4 on the back paws.

3. Cats Can Jump Up To 6 Times Their Own Height

Because cats flexible spines and spring-loaded hind legs, cats have the abilityt to jump super high! The back legs of a cat are actually more powerful then the front. This helps the cat jump really high escpailly since their back leg muscles can provide a burst of energy.

4. Cats Have Fingerprints

Cats acutally do not have fingerprints, however, they have similar patters on the ridges of the noses, similar to human fingers. Thus, if your cat has comitted any crimes, you can tell if they leave a mark with their nose! Case closed.

5. Cats Own Their Personal Hairbrush

A cat's tongue has a hook like texture called papillae, which helps cats groom themselves. This is why when cats lick you, it feels almost like sandpaper!
